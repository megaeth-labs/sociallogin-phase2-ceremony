## Design Context

### Users
Public ceremony contributors on desktop browsers; mobile visitors can read about
the ceremony and inspect public history. Contributors need to understand when
they can participate, what their browser is doing, and what acceptance means.

### Brand Personality
Welcoming, collective, candid. The owner explicitly requested a more expressive,
community-focused direction on 2026-09-24. Make participation feel approachable
without overstating security or activity.

### Aesthetic Direction
As of 2026-09-30, the owner requests stronger MegaETH branding with MOSS Wallet
as the primary reference. Use the wallet's Helvetica Neue, soft gray surfaces
(#f0eeeb / #f5f5f5), forest text (#091a10), deep green controls (#2d5a44),
rounded panels and pill buttons. Reuse the actual MOSS wordmark, MegaETH mark,
and wallet rabbit artwork. Keep the welcoming community message and a clear
participation area, with restrained decoration during active contributions.
Brand consistency takes precedence over the previous poster typography and
asymmetric lime/coral panels. See DESIGN_REFERENCE.md for source provenance.

### Design Principles
- A clear next action at every stage, with waiting distinct from computation.
- Claims of acceptance come only from verified coordinator evidence.
- Show unavailable capabilities honestly; do not simulate real participation.
- Keep cryptographic details available through progressive disclosure.
- Keyboard access, visible focus, responsive reading layouts and reduced motion.

### Night moss direction (2026-10-12)
The owner asked for a homepage modelled on moss-account.vercel.app (large hero,
clear calls to action, engaging ceremony attributes, link footer), the animated
wallet rabbit, a cursor-following bunny on the entropy screen and a generative
bunny collectible, with a restrained cyberpunk edge.
- Tone: the MOSS greenhouse after dark. Calm, tactile, a little nocturnal.
- Type: Helvetica Neue only (brand). Hero in Bold caps, tight leading; body Roman.
- Color: daytime wallet palette for reading surfaces; night tokens
  (`--color-night*`, `--color-neon`, sparing `--color-haze`) for hero, footer,
  entropy field and NFT art. Neon is a highlight, never a fill for large areas.
- Motion: one hero load sequence; otherwise motion answers input. 150–300ms
  ease-out on transform/opacity; the entropy bunny is the one playful exception
  and still stills itself under reduced motion.
- Grid: 78rem container, 12 columns; hero and footer full-bleed inset windows;
  attributes as an asymmetric bento of nested (tray + plate) panels.
